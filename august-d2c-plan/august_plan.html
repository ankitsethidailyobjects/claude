Why tilt to repeat: repeat traffic converts at 1.53% vs 0.63% for new and only ~35% of it needs paid media — so each incremental repeat order is far cheaper than a new one and lands in the high-LTV categories. Guardrail: don't drop new intake below ~2.9 M users or the Sept–Oct repeat pool contracts.

The ask to Performance Marketing

  • 1Acquire 2.97 M new users → 18,762 orders. Concentrate prospecting on Power Bank, Stack & Node (ROAS >3.15, high/med LTV). Cap Watch Bands.
  • 2Drive 1.43 M repeat users → 21,864 orders. CRM + app push + retargeting; skew re-engagement to Power Bank / Stack / Charging Station.
  • 3Shift order mix to 46% new / 54% repeat — improves blend without losing topline.
  • 4Hold blended Meta ROAS ≥ 3.28x (last-4-day bar). Plan lands 3.39x at ₹2.36 cr spend.
  • 5Run 4 remaining freebie weekends for ~₹0.38 cr accretive revenue.
Model: total revenue = (new orders × ₹2,450) + (repeat orders × ₹2,700); users = orders ÷ conversion rate (from daily new-vs-repeat tracker, web+app). Category economics from Meta Ads Jun/Jul 2026 (spend, orders, purchase value); retargeting used as repeat-cost proxy; Meta-attributed revenue assumed ≈76% of total D2C.
