01

Executive summary

The five things that are true, in order of money attached.

1. DailyObjects runs on a two-engine system that the last-click report hides. Meta is the demand-creation engine — 6.8M sessions, the highest traffic, but the lowest store conversion (0.62%) and lowest add-to-cart rate (7.8%). Direct + Brand Search + Email are the demand-harvest engine — Direct alone converts at 5.08% and books 30.5% of revenue while spending nothing. Direct doesn't "just happen." It is overwhelmingly Meta- and brand-induced demand arriving without a tracked referrer. Manage them as one funnel, not two scorecards.

2. We are buying the wrong Meta inventory. 79% of Meta budget sits in always-on "Category" prospecting + broad DPA at 3.4× ROAS and frequency as high as 10.6 (deep fatigue). The two campaign types that actually convert — UGC/Influencer (18.4×) and sale-event bursts (12×) — share only 21% of spend. This is the single largest, fastest lever in the business.

3. The funnel doesn't leak evenly — it leaks at the cart, and only on paid traffic. Across the store, only ~24% of carts become checkouts and the largest single drop is add-to-cart → checkout. But it's concentrated: Direct converts a cart to a buyer 24% of the time; Meta paid social does it 7.9% of the time. Meta sends "browsers in adding mode," not "buyers." The cases category proves it — PDPs used as cold ad landing pages hit 30–45% add-to-cart and <1% purchase.

4. Retention is the cheapest CVR lever we own and it is under-funded. Returning users convert at 2.91% vs 1.06% for new — and CRM (Email/SMS) drives barely 0.5% of revenue. Every point of repeat-rate is worth more than a point of ad efficiency.

5. Timing is a free multiplier. Monday + the weekend produce 2× the daily purchases of Tue–Thu; 9pm–midnight is the buying window (CVR 2.5% at 11pm vs 1.0% at 7am). We are not pacing budget, offers or sends to the clock the customer actually buys on.

The store doesn't have a conversion problem. It has an attribution problem that causes a budget-allocation problem — and a cart that is leakproof for warm traffic and a sieve for cold.

02

Key findings & the surprises

Where the data contradicted the dashboard. Each is rated for what kind of claim it is.

Confirmed winners
  • UGC & Influencer creative is the highest-ROAS asset class by a distance (18.4× vs 3.4×). likely causal
  • Returning & app users convert 2.7–6× better; the GA4 "(not set)" landing bucket (app/deeplink) books ₹15.2 Cr at 6.3% CVR. likely causal
  • Brand Search returns 82× in-sale / 10× steady — pure demand harvesting. correlation
  • Sale events (HPS) lift ROAS 3–4× across every category vs the same campaigns off-sale. likely causal
Confirmed losers & surprises
  • Always-on Category prospecting (51.5% of Meta spend) is stuck at 3.4× with frequency 6–10.6. Fatigued. correlation
  • Collection & campaign landing pages convert worst — /collection 0.39%, /wireless-charger/lp 0.28% — below the homepage (1.86%). correlation
  • Surprise: phone-case PDPs get 30–45% add-to-cart but <1% purchase — high-intent browsing, low-intent buying. likely causal
  • Surprise: Audience Network shows 10.7× ROAS on 21% CTR — almost certainly accidental/incentivised clicks, not incremental sales. flag / verify

Meta deep-dive & the attribution illusion

Not "which campaign has the best ROAS" — why it wins, and why the platform's own numbers can't be taken at face value.

Meta's pixel (7-day click / 1-day view) claims 3.6× more purchases than GA4 gives it on last-click. Both are "right." The truth is in between — and the missing conversions don't vanish, they reappear as Direct (80,734 purchases, 5.08% CVR) and Brand Search (82× ROAS). A customer sees an Instagram Reel, doesn't click-buy, returns two days later by typing "dailyobjects" or the URL, and converts. Last-click hands the medal to Direct; Meta's pixel double-counts it. causal mechanism inferred — only a geo/holdout incrementality test settles the true number (see §11)

*ROAS uses Meta's omni-purchase attribution for comparability across campaigns. 78.7% of budget earns 3.4×; the 12–18× buckets get 21%. Caveat: UGC/event ROAS is partly inflated by sale-period halo and cannot absorb unlimited budget without decaying — but the gradient is unambiguous and large.

Why the winners win — the mechanics
  • Freshness beats fatigue. Winning campaigns run at frequency 2–4; the 3.4× always-on set runs at 6–10.6. Same audiences, decayed creative.
  • UGC lowers CPM and raises CTR simultaneously — Influencer/UGC CPM ₹92–132 & CTR 1.8% vs Category Stack CPM ₹63 but CTR 0.75%. Native content earns cheaper, more-qualified clicks.
  • Offer + urgency closes. Identical category campaigns jump from ~3.4× to 11–14× inside the HPS sale window. The creative is the wrapper; the offer is the trigger.
  • Instagram is the engine — Reels (6.2×), Feed (5.6×), Stories (5.0×) carry ~85% of Meta-driven sales. Facebook feed still pulls 5.2×.
Where to stop the bleed
  • Audience Network — 21% CTR, 10.7× "ROAS" is a red flag, not a win. Almost certainly accidental clicks. Exclude & re-measure. verify
  • Category Stack & Power Bank — ₹20.3 Cr combined spend at freq 6–10.6 and 3.4×. Cap frequency, refresh creative, or cut.
  • Awareness line — ₹1.8 L at 1.2× and no measured downstream lift. Justify with a brand-lift study or reallocate.
  • Core buyer is sharper than we target. Male 25–34 alone books ₹14 Cr at 5.9× pixel-ROAS; 25–44 M+F is the profitable core. Trim 55+ and unknown.

Predictive rules — "score the session like a model would"
  • If returning user + lands via Direct/app + prior purchase in viewed category → purchase probability very high (≈ 5–7× baseline). Prioritise inventory & offer, not persuasion.
  • If session uses on-site search or applies a filter + views 3+ products → CVR rises sharply; suppress discount, push speed-to-cart.
  • If first-touch Meta UGC + second session Brand Search within 72h → high-value harvest; ensure brand SERP + retargeting are live.
  • If cold Meta + collection/PDP LP + new user → lowest probability; this is where offer + social proof must do the work.
  • If cart created 9pm–12am Mon/weekend → highest completion odds; trigger real-time urgency / free-ship nudge.

10

The DailyObjects conversion playbook

25 reusable laws distilled from the analysis — the operating system for Meta, Google, CRM, SEO, LP/PDP, merchandising, pricing & retention.

01

Manage demand as one funnel. Meta creates it; Direct, Brand Search & CRM harvest it. Never optimise a channel in isolation on last-click. ATTRIBUTION

02

Spend follows the ROAS gradient, not the spend habit. If 79% of budget earns the lowest return, the org chart is wrong, not the market. META

03

UGC is an asset class, not a creative test. Native creator content earns cheaper, more-qualified clicks. Build a pipeline, not a one-off. CREATIVE

04

Freshness is a conversion input. Above frequency ~4 with stale creative, ROAS decays. Refresh on a schedule, not on a panic. META

05

The offer is the trigger; the creative is the wrapper. Same ads earn 3–4× more inside a sale. Engineer always-on micro-offers. OFFERS

06

Match landing page to traffic temperature. Cold → single-offer category LP; warm → PDP. Never land cold traffic on /collection. LANDING

07

A high add-to-cart rate is a trap, not a trophy. Cases prove adds ≠ buys. Optimise to purchase, never to ATC. CRO

08

The cart→checkout step is the leak. Only ~24% advance. Surface offers, free-ship thresholds & UPI/EMI at the cart, not after. CHECKOUT

09

Warm traffic doesn't leak. Direct completes carts 3× better than Meta. The cheapest "checkout fix" is sending warmer traffic. CRO

10

Returning > new, always. 2.7× CVR. A point of repeat-rate beats a point of ad efficiency. RETENTION

11

The app is a conversion machine — feed it. App/deeplink sessions convert at 6%+. Drive installs as a CVR strategy. RETENTION

12

CRM is underbuilt. <0.5% of revenue from Email/SMS in a 2.9%-returning business is a gap, not a ceiling. CRM

13

SMS punches above its weight. 6.7% CVR. Reserve it for high-intent, time-boxed triggers. CRM

14

Protect the brand SERP. Brand Search returns 10–82×; it is the harvest point for Meta-created demand. Never let it go dark. GOOGLE/SEO

15

PMax is a harvester, not a creator. 6.9× last-click is partly cannibalised brand/shopping demand. Cap brand inclusion; judge on incrementality. GOOGLE

16

Sell to the clock. 9pm–midnight & Mon/weekend ≈ 2× purchase rate. Daypart budget, sends & drops. MEDIA/CRM

17

Instagram is the engine. Reels + Stories + Feed carry ~85% of Meta sales. Build vertical-video-first. META

18

Beware "too-good" ROAS. 21% CTR / 10× on Audience Network is fraud-shaped. Validate cheap wins before scaling. MEDIA

19

Target the core, exclude the tail. Male 25–44 + female 25–44 is the profitable spine; 55+/unknown drains. AUDIENCE

20

Merchandise two jobs separately. Cases = volume/impulse (₹997); bags/premium = margin (₹2,700). Different LPs, offers & KPIs. MERCH

21

On-site search & filters signal buyers. Sessions that self-serve intent convert higher — make search prominent, suppress discount for them. CRO

22

Awareness must prove itself. 1.2× last-touch is only acceptable with a measured brand-lift or incrementality result. META

23

Desktop is the AOV channel. +55% AOV, 2.6% CVR. Don't mobile-only the experience; merchandise bundles to desktop. CRO

24

Decide budgets on incremental ROAS, not platform ROAS. Until a holdout runs, every reallocation is a hypothesis. Build the test. MEASUREMENT

25

Event-ize the calendar. Sale bursts lift every category 3–4×. A cadence of credible mini-events beats permanent discounting. PRICING

Executive recommendations

What to do Monday, this quarter, and to de-risk the whole thesis.

Now · 0–2 weeks
  • Shift 25% of always-on/DPA budget into UGC/creator + always-on event creative.
  • Frequency-cap Category Stack & Power Bank; queue creative refresh.
  • Exclude Audience Network; re-baseline ROAS.
  • Turn on evening (9pm–12am) + Mon/weekend dayparting.
  • Confirm brand-search coverage is 100% — never let the harvest point lapse.
This quarter
  • Stand up behavioural CRM (browse/cart/replenish) + app-install push program.
  • Ship temperature-matched landing pages; stop landing cold traffic on /collection & PDPs.
  • Cart-stage offer surfacing + one-tap UPI checkout test.
  • Build a credible mini-event calendar to replace permanent discounting.
  • Split merchandising KPIs for impulse (cases) vs margin (bags/premium).
De-risk · measurement
  • Run a Meta geo-holdout to measure incremental ROAS and the true Direct/Brand-Search credit.
  • Holdout-control every CRM flow and the LP/checkout tests.
  • Instrument scroll-depth, search & filter events to confirm the engagement→CVR link.
  • Reconcile GA4 vs platform attribution into one blended MMM view for budget decisions.
